READING.

1. THE MEAN AND THE TAIL NAME DIFFERENT STAGES.  On the SBND gate manifest the biggest
   mean increment is TaggerCheckNeutrino:pr (+0.39) and Steiner is +0.16.  On the five
   doc-116 tail events Steiner is +1.2 to +1.4 GiB -- about 8x its own mean -- while
   TaggerCheckNeutrino stays near +0.29.  A memory target ranked on means would be the
   wrong target for every event that breaks a cap.

2. TaggerCheckNeutrino IS NARROW, STEINER IS HEAVY-TAILED.  The tagger's increment sits
   in a tight band on both the gate arm and the tail events; Steiner's max/mean ratio is
   ~4x on PDHD and ~6x on PDVD.  The stage that varies is the stage that matters.

3. SAME ON ALL THREE DETECTORS.  Steiner has the largest max increment on SBND, PDHD and
   PDVD alike, and the heap profile (119_r4_heap_sbnd.txt) puts CreateSteinerGraph::visit
   at 70-78 % of PEAK LIVE HEAP on both detectors profiled.

4. ROUND 3 DID NOT MOVE MEMORY, as expected -- it was a CPU round.  The d118flip and
   d119r3 gate arms agree to three decimals on every stage.
