Noise floor: for each metric, |s0rep - baseline| (same config, same binary, same input; only the
DL/SCN vertex is not bit-stable).  Reported before any verdict.

Verdict rule per metric x cell (frozen here):
  IMPROVED / DEGRADED  iff  |delta| > 2 x noise floor (and > 0.5 percentage point for a metric whose
                       floor is 0)  AND  the exact two-sided sign test on the paired per-interaction
                       exchange (signal rows selected in baseline only vs cell only; for M2/M4/M6 the
                       background rows; for M7/M8 the matched rows) gives p < 0.05.
  NOT SEPARABLE        otherwise.  "Not separable" is never written as "unchanged".
  For M9/M10 (1000 gates, 5 events) only a change of >= 5 gates counts, and it is reported as a rate
  with its Wilson interval, never as a verdict.
Overall: the trajectory is recommended for SBND only if no primary metric is DEGRADED on tfull and at
least one is IMPROVED.  A mixed result is reported as mixed.

Secondary (reported, not graded): Q1 by true class and Edep bin; the doc-115 miss taxonomy; the
score-scan curves (efficiency/purity vs cut) baseline vs tfull; flash-time association (V11);
exposure-weighted numuCC purity at f = 1 and the common-exposure nueCC purity (doc 115 sec 13
method, the cell's own beam-off rate); the trajectory-closure clauses of pr150_traj_eval.py
(R2D_W, P2, uncov, jitter) on the MC -- the "fit closes better" half of doc pr/150, now on MC.

Degradation follow-up (only where a metric is DEGRADED on tfull or csp3bw):
  origin: truth-adjudicated mover taxonomy (CHOICE / STRUCTURE / LOST-CANDIDATE as doc pr/150 sec 4,
  by true class, Edep bin, cathode band, TPC edges) and first-failing-stage attribution for lost signal
  (no candidate -> vertex > 5 cm -> reco vertex outside FV -> score below cut), with the moved BDT
  inputs on the same interaction.
  improvement attempt: at most TWO rungs from EXISTING keys, chosen by the taxonomy from
  {steiner_terminal_min_separation=0.7 (stub branches at the vertex),
   retile_sampler_charge_threshold=6000 (sampler density),
   dl_vtx_min_accept_score=4.0 (DL admission; pr/150 found it makes the vertex WORSE)},
  each a FULL cell on the degraded sample(s) (never a loser-only manifest -- the conditioning trap).
  A rung PASSES iff every primary metric is >= its tfull value - noise floor AND the degraded metric
  recovers >= half of its loss (baseline - tfull).  No key default and no constant is changed; a rung
  that fails is reported as failing.
