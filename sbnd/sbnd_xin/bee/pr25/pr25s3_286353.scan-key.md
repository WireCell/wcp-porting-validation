The 271 cm main track went **electron → muon**, which is what the owner's
2026-08-03 hand-scan called it (10/10 tracks). So the score drop is **not**
the main track being mis-PID'd — that part is now right.

What moved with it: 9002 flipped `flag_shower` 0.00 → 1.00, a new 2-point
π± segment 9007 appeared, and `kine_reco_Enu_MeV` went 624.9 → 908.1. The
event now presents as *long muon + two small EM fragments* rather than *one
long shower*, and the numu BDT scores that topology lower.

## The question for the scan

Are the small EM fragments (9002, 9003) and the 2-point 9007 real, or
reconstruction debris now being promoted because the trunk no longer absorbs
them? If debris, the numu BDT is reacting to an artifact and the drop is not
a physics regression. If real, this is a genuine BDT-response question about
a topology the training set may under-represent.

Either way it is a BDT/fragmentation question, not a shower-topology one —
`segment_is_shower_topology` is doing what §3 established it should.
