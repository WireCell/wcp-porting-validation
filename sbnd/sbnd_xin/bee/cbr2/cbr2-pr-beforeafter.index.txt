THESE ARE DATA EVENTS (owner, 2026-08-17).  There is no truth to appeal to, and
no guarantee any of them contains a neutrino: in data most in-beam bundles are
cosmic background.  Whether each label change below is a purification or a loss
is a hand-scan call -- that is what these two sets are for.

HEADLINE.  5 of 9 events LOSE THE NEUTRINO CANDIDATE ENTIRELY -- PR selects no
main cluster at all where it previously selected one.  The mechanism is the same
every time: the rescue joins the two cathode halves, the in-beam main gets
LONGER, at 248-429 cm it reaches through-going/stopping-muon size, the cosmic
taggers fire, and nu_skip_cosmic then discards the ONLY in-window main.  Full
analysis, including the owner's three questions on 65289 / 78242 / 51128, is
docs/73 section 11.

SCORE ON THE SELECTION, NOT ON THE LABEL.  nusel-evt<ID>.tsv's `label` column and
TaggerCheckNeutrino's selection are different notions of "main" (unmerge_bundle
renumbers between them -- run_pr_chain_batch.sh's own header says the two are not
joinable).  Reading `label` mis-scores 65053 (label says nu-candidate -> STM, but
PR selects the SAME main L 113.9 cm in both arms) and 281165 (label says
no-bundle, but PR does select a neutrino -- the bundle just moved from flash gid
0 / APA0 to gid 1000007 / APA1, the same 0.86 us scintillation).

NOT ALL LOSSES ARE WRONG.  398115 and 237798 join a far half that is ITSELF in
the beam window -- genuine cathode-crossing muons, 415/417 cm, TGM is correct and
the pre-fix nu-candidate was the artifact.  51128 (a 3.8 cm donor kills a 57.7 cm
neutrino) and 78242 (trajectory fit broken over 40 cm) are not defensible.

LAYERS.  The 4 events that still have a selected neutrino candidate in the AFTER

THE WORST CASE, 51128 (bee 6), read from the rescue's own log:
  c11 (gid 1000004, t0 1.670 us, 3.8 cm) + c8 (gid 2, t0 6.680 us, 283.9 cm)
  -> gid 1000004, rule new-path-beam.
The beam-side donor is a 3.8 cm FRAGMENT, not the bundle's neutrino -- that is a
DIFFERENT cluster, L 57.7 cm, which the OFF arm selects as the neutrino main.
F4 (rescue_dest_beam_for_new) puts the 287.1 cm result in the beam bundle,
longest-cluster-wins makes it the bundle main, the real neutrino is demoted to
associated, TGM tags the merged main, and nu_skip_cosmic_bundle then skips the
remaining in-window clusters because they share the flash bundle with a
cosmic-tagged main.  The neutrino is not even in the skip log -- demotion removed
it from the candidate list before the veto ran.  A 3.8 cm fragment decided the
fate of a 57.7 cm neutrino interaction.  This is the F2 regression risk the
round-2 plan named ("a wrong merge re-stamps a cosmic with the beam T0"), now
visible for the first time because PR was out of scope during the round.  It is
NOT the false-merge bug fixed by the containment veto -- these events pass that
veto cleanly, nothing crosses the cathode.
