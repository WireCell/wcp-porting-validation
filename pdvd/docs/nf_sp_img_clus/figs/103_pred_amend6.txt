doc pdvd/103 -- pre-registration, amendment 6 (2026-09-15): the symmetric check on PDVD before any flip.
Written after the own103v re-grade (sec 12.2: D1 on all four metrics) had been read, and before any item of the check
below is drawn, built or looked at. It is NOT blind to the re-grade. Amendments 1-5 stay in force.

1. Why
   * own103v could only clear false positives (amendment 2 sec 5, the one-sidedness). The PDVD D1 rests on those
     clearances: is_stm FP A0 9 -> 5, A1 16 -> 5; tier 1 changed 29 of 32 labels, all 17 carried-record ones among
     them, while 0 of 8 controls moved.
   * Amendment 4 sec 6's clause ("if D1 depends on 2 or fewer items") is not triggered by its letter: the fewest
     relabels that undo D1 are 6 (is_stm purity) and 7 (Michel purity), figs/103_d1_margin.txt.
   * The check is added as a gate anyway. The review showed the labels are weakest where the two arms disagree, and
     the stoppers tagged by one arm only (not reviewed) sit in that same disagreement set.

2. Population (d103_union_grade.population; cells d103v0 / d103v1; truth own103v > carried record > smx11)
   * A TP-mover is a judged item whose truth is not an owner label (truth source not owner_review, and carried
     confidence not "owner") and that is hand-positive on a metric and tagged on that metric in exactly one cell:
     - is_stm: a hand stopper with is_stm 1 in exactly one of A0 / A1;
     - michel_found: a hand STM_MICHEL with michel_found 1 in exactly one of A0 / A1.
   * Stratum A1: TP-movers tagged in A1 only (on either metric). Stratum A0: tagged in A0 only. An item that is in
     both (one metric each way) belongs to stratum A1.

3. The served set, tag own103v2 (free: no label dir, no record)
   * 10 items from stratum A1 and 10 from stratum A0, each drawn with random.Random(106).sample over the sorted keys.
     A stratum with fewer than 10 items is taken whole and not filled from the other.
   * 4 controls, random.Random(106) over the sorted judged non-owner items that are candidates in both cells with
     identical (is_stm, michel_found), is_stm 1, and not in own103v.
   * 24 items, shuffled with random.Random(106), shown on the A1 payload where the item is an A1 candidate, else A0,
     behind the own103v question panel with the tag renamed. No chain answer, prior label or role on screen.
   * The builder writes the drawn keys only into the set dir; they are not printed, read or graded before the owner
     has labelled the set.

4. Fold and reading
   * own103v2 sits above own103v. Re-grade (a) exactly as sec 12.2.
   * Per stratum and metric, among the labelled items that were TP-movers on that metric:
     - relabel-negative rate r_neg: the owner's verdict is hand-negative on that metric (is_stm: a non-stopper;
       michel_found: not STM_MICHEL);
     - removal rate r_rm: MESSY or UNCLEAR.
     Each is reported with a 68 % Clopper-Pearson interval.
   * Projection: the point rates are applied to that stratum's unreviewed TP-movers on that metric (expected counts,
     not rounded). A relabel-negative moves the tagging cell TP -> FP and the other cell FN -> TN; a removal takes
     the item out of both cells.
   * PDVD D1 is stated only if every metric has A1 - A0 >= -0.020 on BOTH the folded grade (a) and the projection.
     - Folded grade passes, projection fails: "not settled"; a wider review of the failing stratum is proposed.
     - Folded grade fails: D2.
   * Controls: 2 or more of the 4 change stopper class -> "not settled".

5. Unchanged: metrics, thresholds, bootstrap, the D1/D2 rule of 103_pred.txt. PDHD is out of scope (D2 on Michel
   purity, owner-backed, sec 12.3).
