doc pdvd/104 -- pre-registration, amendment 7 (2026-09-15): the symmetric check on PDHD's Michel purity.
Written after doc 104 sec 2 and sec 3 had been read (no admission cut separates the A1-only Michel false positives
from the true Michels; the mechanism is trajectory-end truncation), and before any item of the check below is drawn,
built or looked at. It is NOT blind to those sections. Doc 103's figs/103_pred.txt and amendments 1-6 stay in force;
this amendment governs PDHD only and changes none of their thresholds.

1. Why
   * The PDHD grade of doc 103 sec 10.3 compares a REVIEWED A1 side against an UNREVIEWED A0 side. Of the Michel
     movers, own103h judged 8 of the 33 A1-only tags and 0 of the 21 A0-only tags
     (figs/103_union_grade_pdhd_own103h_stmonlyneg.txt, amendment 2 split (c)).
   * own103h was built as a false-positive adjudication queue (amendment 2 sec 2), so the one-sidedness is by
     construction, and the cost landing on the reviewed subset is expected, not evidence of a bias. What is NOT
     established is what the same reviewer would do to the A0 side.
   * The untouched-population split reads michel purity -0.011 and efficiency +0.028 on 179 of 196 items (same fig),
     i.e. the -0.051 lives entirely in the 17 reviewed items. That is a sensitivity signal, not a rescue.
   * figs/103_d1_margin.txt: PDHD Michel purity is 3 labels from passing. With 21 A0-only tags unreviewed, both the
     current D2 and a possible D1 rest inside that margin.
   * Amendment 6 established the remedy for exactly this shape on PDVD. This amendment applies it to PDHD.

2. Scope
   * Michel only. Both is_stm metrics already pass (-0.017, -0.015, doc 103 sec 10.3) and no is_stm mover is drawn.
   * This amendment does NOT generalise any own103h ruling to an unlabelled item (amendment 2 sec 5). Every verdict
     below is the owner's own call on the item in front of them.
   * Amendment 5's convention is in force: the grade is read with --stm-only-unset-negative, so an owner STM_ONLY
     with no michel_kind is Michel-negative. population() is called with stm_only_unset_negative=True.

3. Population (d103_union_grade.population; cells A0 d101hnew / A1 d102hcs; truth own103h > smx27 > smx28)
   * A Michel TP-mover is a judged item whose truth is not an owner label (source not owner_review / owner, and
     confidence not "owner"), that is hand-Michel-positive, and whose michel_found is 1 in exactly one of A0 / A1.
   * Stratum A1: tagged in A1 only. Stratum A0: tagged in A0 only. The strata are disjoint by construction.
   * The check is symmetric in effect: a relabel-negative in stratum A1 ADDS an A1 false positive (the delta gets
     worse); in stratum A0 it ADDS an A0 false positive (the delta gets better). It can move the grade either way.

4. The served set, tag own103h2 (free: no label dir, no record, no verdicts file)
   * 10 items from stratum A1 and 10 from stratum A0, each drawn with random.Random(107).sample over the sorted
     keys. A stratum with fewer than 10 items is taken whole and not filled from the other.
   * 4 controls, random.Random(107) over the sorted judged non-owner items that are candidates in both cells with
     identical (is_stm, michel_found), is_stm 1, and not in own103h.
   * Shuffled with random.Random(107), shown on the A1 payload where the item is an A1 candidate, else A0, behind
     the own103h question panel with the tag renamed. No chain answer, prior label, stratum or role on screen.
   * The drawn keys and their roles are written only into <out>/items.tsv and are not read before the owner labels.

5. The fold and the rates (d104_tpmover_score.py, written and smoke-tested before any label exists)
   * An own103h2 verdict outranks every other source for that item. MESSY / UNCLEAR removes the item.
   * Per stratum: the relabel-negative rate (hand-positive items the owner calls Michel-negative, including an
     STM_ONLY with no kind under amendment 5) and the removal rate (MESSY / UNCLEAR), each with a 68 % Clopper-
     Pearson interval.
   * The projection applies each stratum's point rate to that stratum's UNREVIEWED movers and re-grades.

6. The decision
   * PDHD D1 only if every one of the four metrics is >= A0 - 0.020 on BOTH the folded grade and the projection.
     Otherwise D2 stands, PDHD keeps production's trajectory, and the two levers are not flipped on PDHD.
   * Controls: 2 or more of 4 stopper-class changes => not settled; the fold is reported and the grade is not
     restated.
   * A pass is reported to the owner with the numbers. It is not itself authority to change a production default.

7. What this amendment does not do
   * It does not adopt the L2b conn-1 length floor of doc 104 sec 2.4. That lever clears the arithmetic bar on both
     detectors but was found by scanning the same data it would be graded on, leaves 4 of the 6 A1-only false
     positives standing, discards 1.5 (PDHD) and 4.3 (PDVD) true Michels per false positive removed, and would hand
     back PDVD's banked Michel efficiency. Adopting it would need its own pre-registration and an out-of-sample
     grade; neither is in scope here.
