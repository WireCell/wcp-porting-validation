MicroBooNE, which has many more dead channels).  Does removing the retile -- keeping the charge_stepped sampling --
fix the off-image seed problem (docs 111, 112) without introducing more issues?

    L3 none       no tiling: each original blob re-sampled in place with the retile samplers (charge_stepped)  <- the ask
    L2 footprint  re-tile the cluster's own blob footprints only (no dead / nearby-charge extension, no painting)
    L1 no_paint   the full activity (footprints + dead + good charge within 20 cm), no path-disc painting
  L1 and L2 attribute the effect; L3 is the owner's question.  No other mode or setting is tried in this round.

PREDICTIONS (stated before measuring; not gating):
  * L3 removes doc 112's mechanism: the 1blank share of non-extreme terminals falls from the base value to near zero
    (only a blob footprint wire with no CTPC charge can still give a zero plane without a dead bit).
  * L3 does NOT remove zero-charge planes: a dead wire inside an original blob is written (0, 1e12) and charge_stepped
    with disable_mix_dead_cell=false admits a zero-charge wire (doctest_retile_mode.cxx case 4), so part of the 1blank
    population becomes 1dead, and the 1dead terminals are already 25.0 % (PDHD) / 22.3 % (PDVD) > 1 cm off at base.
    If S1 misses its bar, this is the first place to look.  "none" means no new blobs, not no zero-charge cells.
  * far (> 5 cm) seed deviations are graph-level bridges (doc 111 r2) and do not shrink; they may grow where the
    retile's fill had carried the path across a dead region.
  * tags churn: doc 102 r2 lost 0.18 STM efficiency (PDHD) from a trajectory-improving change; the base->arm candidate
    churn is expected to be at least that of the accepted charge_stepped flips (reference numbers below).
