OWNER REQUEST (2026-09-18): study the nature of the PDHD and PDVD tag regressions on the alpha-0.5 trajectory, retune
the STM and Michel taggers on both, and if the validation passes turn on the knobs for both the trajectory and the
taggers.  Owner decisions taken before this rule: (1) PDVD carries BWP05 (tree+path alpha 0.5 WITHOUT prefer3) when

WHAT THE STUDY FOUND (sec 2; the rule rests on it):
  * The tagger's inputs did not move coherently: on the common candidates plateau_med B/A p50 1.000, ks_mu - ks_flat
    shift p50 0.000, muon_len B/A 1.000 (p10/p90 within 6-20 %).  80 % of the common candidates keep both tags; the
    20 % that change sit at |d stop| 1.6-2.2 cm (p50) against 0.3 cm for the unchanged.  The regressions are boundary
    flips of decisions the tagger was already sitting on, not a scale shift -- so no re-centring of the plateau / KS
    windows is warranted and none is attempted.
  * Symmetric channels (lost ~ gained, no threshold fixes them): the STM evaluation (status 0 <-> 3, prototype
    constants, no config key), the Bragg / shape bits (no_bragg | shape_flat), mid-point / left-charge exits.
  * One-way channels with an existing config key (the levers):
      L1  PDVD: 10 hand stoppers lost to detect_proton (status 0 -> 5; 5 of them with an attached Michel of 8-42 MeV)
          against 2 gained.  TaggerCheckSTM's proton_muon_guard (doc 63 round 2: "an end matching the muon hypothesis
          in shape and normalisation is not called a proton", ks1 < 0.040 and ratio3 < 1.1) is OFF in both
          productions (driver stm_proton_muon_guard=false; SBND production true).  PDHD lost 1 / gained 1.
      L2  The Michel arm at the stop re-classified: kind Michel -> other when the kink at the stop fell below
          michel_min_kink_deg 30 (PDVD 039349_10/66 39.6 -> 22.6 deg, 039252_12/117 30.8 -> 26.9; PDHD 028084_21/132
          44.3 -> 24.9) or the arm + its far subtree exceeded michel_max_len_cm 25 (PDVD 039349_50/49 28.0 cm).
          Sized on the ELIGIBLE population of the alpha-0.5 arms (accepted stoppers without a Michel, all their
          stop arms): kink >= 20 admits PDVD 3 (3 hand-positive), PDHD 2 (1 pos / 1 neg); len <= 30 admits PDVD 3
          (3 pos), PDHD 1 (1 pos).  Nothing else is eligible: the "no stop-arm at all" class (PDVD 8, PDHD most)
          has no key.  The Michel's Steiner terminals are NOT dropped by the trajectory (B/A 0.97 vs control 0.98).
      L3  The range-energy veto (michel_range_energy_guard, ke_min 10 MeV) on a Michel piece that the new trajectory
          detached from the stop (conn 1 -> 2/3, dis 3-15 cm): PDVD 5 hand-positive lost; ke_min 5 admits PDVD 6
          (4 pos / 2 neg), PDHD 0.
  * The Michel FALSE POSITIVES gained (PDVD 10, PDHD 4) come through the symmetric channels and through very small
    arms (0.5-2 cm, 1.6-2.3 MeV) at the stop; no existing key removes them without an equal cut on true Michels
    (doc 104: an energy or length floor cuts both classes at the same rate).  No floor is built.

THE BLIND SCAN (sec 5): every candidate of A0, T or any R_k of a detector that has no truth of any source, plus 10 %
  calibration items drawn from the judged record (V2 agreement check of d103_scan_record.py); prep on the arms
  themselves (scratch /home/xqian/tmp/d116/round), shots through the doc-103 headless viewer, one blind Opus agent per
  wave under the frozen rubrics (PDHD pdhd_stm_michel_scan_rubric.md sha 750751ea..., PDVD d99/swap_scan_rubric.md
  sha d760e223...), records through mkv.py only, every transcript audited (d103_audit.py), record tag smx116 (new;
  never overwriting).  The display arm of an item is the cell where it is a candidate (A0 first, then T, then R_k).

DECISION per level, both detectors, read AFTER the fold:
  PASS  every one of the four metrics >= A0 - 0.020 on the labelled point value AND its NEG bound (every remaining
        unlabelled candidate forced negative) >= -0.030, on BOTH detectors;
        AND no-regression: C (wire-cell rc 0 and the three outputs for every event; a "no candidate" event is
        complete), R (median per-event PR wall / the T arm <= 1.20), G0 (pin md5 2efa7fa09325 before and after);
        AND the split-half guard: on the odd- and even-indexed halves of the event manifest separately, no metric
        of the level < A0 - 0.040 (against overfitting the 61 / 120 events the levers were sized on).
  Otherwise FAIL, naming the failing clauses per detector.
  SELECTION = the smallest ladder level (R1 < R2 < R3) that PASSES on both detectors; it is not the best number.
  If no level passes on both: NOTHING IS FLIPPED; the doc reports the per-detector reading of every level, the
  fewest-failing level, and the recommended next step; the trajectory knobs stay OFF as well (the owner tied the
  two flips together).

PREDICTIONS (not gating):
  * R1 recovers most of PDVD's proton-channel losses (+5..8 is_stm TP, +3..5 Michel TP) if the guard fires on ends
    that match the muon hypothesis; +-1 on PDHD.  It adds no false positive that the study can see (protons are rare
    in these cosmic samples; the guard only un-vetoes).
  * R2 adds +2..3 Michel TP per detector and <= 1 FP (PDHD 029107_19/111 is hand-negative and eligible at kink 24).
  * R3 adds +3..4 Michel TP and +2 FP on PDVD, nothing on PDHD.
  * PDHD: is_stm both metrics pass at every level; Michel purity is within one false positive of the bar (-0.024
    NEG / -0.011 POS today) and is decided by the blind fold and by whether R2's one eligible negative is admitted.
  * PDVD: is_stm passes at R1+; Michel efficiency reaches the bar at R2 or R3 (needs TP >= 167 of 236 after the
    fold); Michel PURITY IS PREDICTED TO FAIL AT EVERY LEVEL (FP 18 vs 11; the levers add true positives and remove
    no false positive; purity >= 0.920 needs FP <= 14 at TP ~ 175).  If so the round ends without a flip and the
    honest next step is the false-positive side (a re-scan of the 10 new Michel FPs on the new trajectory, 5 of
    which carry blind-agent THRU labels, or a Michel-object floor built and graded out of sample).
  * The bwp05 trajectory (Part 1) changes the PDVD T cell's FP set; the prediction above is for P3BWP05.
