# wcfm — Wire-Cell foundation model (FM) integration

Runner scripts, jsonnet forks and docs for bringing the trained 2D foundation model
(`WC_FM_DINO`, DUNE FD-HD 1x2x6 "workspace" sim) into the toolkit chain. Design inputs live in
`wc-pr-ml-thoughts/` (GNN blob-deghosting design, PR-ML ideas, TriCross study, owner slides).

Consequence: build the stage on charge for the non-isochronous regime; the 0° slab needs a global or three-view method |
| [06_iso-slab-missing-link.md](06_iso-slab-missing-link.md) | Why the 0° (isochronous) slab is unsolved by the doc 05 chain, and the missing link (2026-09-25, analysis only; `iso_slab_probe.py`). A 0° track is 2–4 identical slices of a filled hexagon of candidates (4–30 per real cell). **Source 2 (charge matching) has nothing to match**: the sample's dQ/dx is constant by construction (`CHARGE_PER_STEP`, `TrackDepos` has no fluctuation), a three-view consistency statistic is at the prior (AP/prior 1.0–1.6) while at 0.5° the slice-splitting alone makes it 2–7×. **Source 3 (FM) cannot associate views**: the wire descriptor is translation-invariant along the track (cos ≥ 0.96 at 5 wires, 0.90–0.99 at 20), so ghosts — made of real pixels — pool the same descriptors; it could act only at landmarks, which the sample lacks. The graph has no in-slice or wire–wire edges, the wire update has no charge-conservation residual, the per-cell BCE learns the marginal (P(real) 0.05–0.32 for real, 0.02–0.24 for ghosts), and the legacy solver sums the per-wire rows into 4–12 measures per slice before solving. A 40-line truth-free geometric rule (the hexagon chord covering all three pitch intervals) recovers 0.60–1.00 of the true charge on 10 of 13 zero-degree anode-events (GNN median 0.18, legacy 0.06): the information is there and global. Redesign: E1 textured/landmark simulation with go/no-go on the consistency statistic and a sequence-alignment probe; E2 in-slice + wire adjacency, an unrolled conservation channel and a projection + continuity loss with three classical controls; E3 the FM re-test on landmarks with the 0° stratum as the endpoint |
